1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY
This protocol governs corrective action, formal Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs), 
and separation agreements for underperforming employees. All processes outlined herein 
must be conducted in close collaboration with the designated People Business Partner (PBP).

2. STAGED CORRECTIVE ACTION FRAMEWORK
- Stage 1: Written Feedback & 30-Day Informal Coaching
  * Prior to formal PIP placement, the manager must provide specific, documented written feedback 
    identifying deficiencies, concrete deliverables, and weekly 1:1 check-ins over a 30-day window.
- Stage 2: Formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP - 60 Days)
  * If performance does not recover to 'Meets High Standards', the employee is placed on a formal 60-day PIP.
  * The PIP document must contain SMART goals, weekly milestones, and explicit criteria for successful completion.
  * Day 30 Checkpoint: Manager and PBP conduct a formal mid-term evaluation.
  * Day 60 Final Determination: Successful completion restores employee to standard standing; 
    unsuccessful completion leads to separation.

3. OPTIONAL MUTUAL SEPARATION TRANSITION PACKAGE
At the initiation of a formal PIP, employees in good behavioral standing may be offered a voluntary Mutual Separation Option 
in lieu of entering the 60-day PIP process:
- Standard Voluntary Transition Consideration:
  * 6 weeks of base salary continuation.
  * 2 months of employer-subsidized COBRA health insurance coverage.
  * Accelerated vesting of any RSU tranches scheduled to vest within 45 days of the separation date.
- Condition: Requires execution of a standard mutual general release and non-disparagement agreement.

4. INVOLUNTARY SEPARATION & SEVERANCE SCHEDULE
Where involuntary separation occurs following an unsuccessful PIP or organizational redundancy:
- Severance Pay Scale:
  * IC1 - IC3: 2 weeks of base salary per completed full year of continuous service (minimum 4 weeks, maximum 16 weeks).
  * IC4 - IC5 & M1 - M2: 3 weeks of base salary per year of service (minimum 8 weeks, maximum 26 weeks).
  * Vice Presidents & Executive Officers: Governed by individual executive employment agreements (minimum 26 weeks).
- Equipment Retrieval:
  * Company laptop and security badges must be surrendered within 24 hours of notification. 
  * IT Service Desk initiates automated remote wipe via Jamf/InTune upon separation meeting completion.
