1. COMPENSATION PHILOSOPHY & MARKET POSITIONING
Nova Technologies targets the 75th percentile of the Radford Global Technology Compensation Survey 
for base salaries and the 85th percentile for total target compensation (Cash + Annual Bonus + Equity). 
Compensation reviews occur bi-annually in June and December.

Tier-3 locations receive a 0.82 geographic differential.

4. RESTRICTED STOCK UNIT (RSU) VESTING SCHEDULES
- Standard 4-Year Vesting Schedule:
  * 25% of total grant vests on the 1-year anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date (the "One-Year Cliff").
  * The remaining 75% vests in equal quarterly installments over the subsequent 36 months (6.25% per quarter).
- Acceleration Clauses:
  * Double-Trigger Acceleration applies upon a Change of Control (acquisition) if an executive is terminated without cause within 12 months.

5. PERFORMANCE MULTIPLIERS (BI-ANNUAL REVIEW)
Annual corporate performance and individual ratings dictate the final bonus payout:
- Rating 5 (Significantly Exceeds Expectations - Top 10% Bell Curve):
  * Bonus Multiplier: 130% to 150% of target payout. Fast-track promotion eligibility.
- Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations - Next 25%):
  * Bonus Multiplier: 110% to 125% of target payout.
- Rating 3 (Meets High Standards - Middle 50%):
  * Bonus Multiplier: 100% of target payout.
- Rating 2 (Partially Meets - Bottom 10%):
  * Bonus Multiplier: 50% of target payout. Mandatory 60-day coaching plan.
- Rating 1 (Unsatisfactory - Bottom 5%):
  * Bonus Multiplier: 0%. Immediate Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or separation.
