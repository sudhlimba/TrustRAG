1. CRISIS GOVERNANCE & SOVEREIGN DECISION AUTHORITY
In the event of an existential cyber attack, ransomware extortion event, or critical infrastructure compromise, 
command authority shifts immediately to the Executive Cyber Defense Council (ECDC):
- ECDC Quorum: Chief Executive Officer (David Vance), Chief Technology Officer, General Counsel, and CISO.
- If CEO is unavailable, decision authority passes sequentially to: (1) General Counsel, (2) VP of Engineering.

2. RANSOM EXTORTION & PAYMENT POLICY
- Strict Default Stance: Nova Technologies maintains a strict policy AGAINST making extortion or ransom payments 
  to threat actors or unauthorized entities.
- OFAC Sanctions Compliance: Under US Department of the Treasury (OFAC) advisories, payments to designated cyber criminal 
  syndicates (e.g. LockBit, BlackCat/ALPHV) constitute civil and criminal federal violations.
- Emergency Exception Protocol:
  * Only in a life-safety catastrophe or total unrecoverable loss of critical customer infrastructure may ransom negotiation 
    be explored.
  * Any extortion payment requires 100% unanimous affirmative written consent of the Board of Directors, 
    engagement of an authorized forensic negotiator (e.g., Mandiant / Unit 42), and formal notification to the FBI Cyber Division.

3. REGULATORY NOTIFICATION MANDATES & 4-DAY SEC CLOCK
Under the SEC Cybersecurity Disclosure Rule (Form 8-K Item 1.05):
- Materiality Determination: The ECDC must convene within 2 hours of discovery to evaluate whether the incident is "material" 
  to shareholders, considering financial, operational, and reputational impact.
- Filing Timeline: Once an incident is determined material, Nova Tech is legally mandated to file Form 8-K with the SEC 
  within 4 business days.
- Department of Justice (DOJ) National Security Delay: Only the US Attorney General can authorize a 30-day delay in public filing 
  if public disclosure poses an immediate risk to national security.

4. AIR-GAPPED DISASTER RECOVERY & COLD BACKUP RESTORATION
In the event that production AWS VPC environments are completely compromised or encrypted:
- S3 Object Lock & Immutable Vault:
  * Database daily snapshots are mirrored to an isolated, air-gapped AWS account with S3 Object Lock in 'COMPLIANCE' mode 
    (WORM - Write Once, Read Many). Even root AWS credentials cannot delete or encrypt snapshots during the 90-day retention lock.
- Clean-Room Rebuild Sequence:
  1. Spin up a greenfield AWS environment using air-gapped Terraform state stored in physical hardware security modules (HSM).
  2. Restore PostgreSQL Aurora databases from the verified immutable S3 snapshots.
  3. Validate database cryptographic SHA-256 hash chains to ensure zero database tampering occurred prior to the breach.
  4. Perform vulnerability patch verification before re-attaching public ingress routes.
