CFO and VP of Finance presented the financial report for the close of Q3:

  * Q3 Recognized ARR: $41,400,000 (representing 46% YoY top-line growth).

  * Gross Margin: Maintained at 81.2% after optimizing cloud compute node pools.

- Timing: Formal investor roadshow slated for Q1 2027.

4. LITIGATION UPDATE & PATENT DEFENSE CONTINGENCY
General Counsel Michael Ross briefed the Board on the ongoing patent infringement dispute filed by Sentinel Systems, LLC:
- Summary: Sentinel claims Nova Tech's zero-trust hierarchical vector query engine infringes US Patent No. 10,842,119.
- Legal Assessment: Outside counsel (Kirkland & Ellis) assesses probability of adverse judgment at less than 15%, 
  citing substantial prior art in Apache Lucene and Milvus metadata filtering algorithms.
- Reserve Allocation: The Board unanimously approved establishing a $4,500,000 litigation defense and potential settlement reserve.

5. EXECUTIVE SESSION & RETENTION MATTERS
Non-management directors met in executive session:
- Approved the FY26 executive bonus pool allocation at 112% of target based on strong Enterprise SaaS revenue beats.
- Confirmed approval for the Project Titan acquisition term sheet for Aegis Data Labs at $145,000,000, 
  authorizing CEO David Vance to execute binding documentation.
