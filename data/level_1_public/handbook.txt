# NOVA TECH ENTERPRISE - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & GENERAL POLICIES
Clearance Required: Level 1 (Intern / Public Access)

1. OFFICE HOURS & REMOTE WORK:
Standard core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday.
Employees and interns are eligible for hybrid work (up to 2 days remote per week) with supervisor approval.

2. HOLIDAY CALENDAR:
The company observes all major federal holidays, plus an annual year-end company shutdown from December 24 through January 1.
All interns and full-time team members receive paid time off during these dates.

3. CAFETERIA & OFFICE AMENITIES:
- Lunch is served daily on Floor 2 from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM.
- Coffee bars and snack pantries on all floors are open 24/7.
- Building gym and wellness rooms are accessible with any standard employee ID badge.

4. IT SUPPORT & GENERAL FAQS:
- IT Helpdesk is located on Floor 1, Room 104.
- Email: it-support@novatech.internal
- For forgotten passwords or badge replacements, bring photo ID to reception.
- Public Wi-Fi network: 'NovaTech-Guest' (Password changes on the 1st of every month).
